WHAT FILL PRICE THE RESOLVER BOOKS, PER CONFIRMATION PATH
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
In ALL FOUR confirmation paths the booked entry price is IDENTICAL: the stored SIGNAL ENTRY
PRICE (entryFillPrice = signal.entryPrice, :258). The confirming bar influences WHETHER entry
is credited, never AT WHAT PRICE. Consequences:

* PATH zone        (band touched at ±$1.0 tol): entry booked = signal.entryPrice even though the
  actual touch happened somewhere inside [entryMin−1, entryMax+1]. A real market order at that
  moment would have filled at the TRADED price of that touch — within ±$1 of the band edge,
  often exactly the band edge of the FARTHER side from TP (adverse) rather than entryPrice.
* PATH touchedExtended (±$3.0 tol): ditto, but the touch can be up to $3 away from entryPrice.
  Booked as entryPrice. On gold (PIP=$0.10), $3 = 30 pips ≈ large fraction of an $8 SL (80 pips).
* PATH crossedTp1  (bar hit TP1, band NEVER touched): entry booked = entryPrice SIMULTANEOUSLY
  WITH the TP level being marked hit ON THE SAME BAR (the confirmation `continue` falls straight
  into ladder evaluation on the SAME bar iteration). The ladder walk then evaluates TP/SL on
  that very bar — i.e., the trade is treated as having been entered in time to catch levels the
  confirming bar itself printed. A market order placed AFTER seeing those prints could not have
  obtained those fills retroactively.
* PATH crossedSl   (bar hit SL first, band never touched): symmetric mechanical defect, the
  worst one: the trade is entered at entryPrice on a bar that had ALREADY printed the stop
  level, and the pre-TP1 branch (:383-390) fires only if a LATER bar breaches slTriggerPrice —
  so the first bar's printed violation is consumed as the entry event itself. If later bars
  recover, the book records a win whose position, filled honestly at the NEXT bar's open (or
  at the printed-violation price under any sane execution), starts under water.

isBuy/sell note: the two touchedZone arms and two touchedExtended arms are ALGEBRAICALLY
IDENTICAL expressions reordered (`low<=hiEdge && high>=loEdge` both ways) — direction does not
change the test; only crossedTp1/crossedSl flip sides.

THE FIX (expo/app/_layout.tsx, web-only, installed once before React renders):
A scoped Node.prototype.removeChild guard:
- NORMAL case (child.parentNode === this): untouched original call — exact
  previous behaviour, zero risk to correct paths.
- PATHOLOGICAL case (child re-parented or detached behind React's back):
  remove it from wherever it actually lives (or treat as already removed when
  detached) and let the commit COMPLETE instead of crashing the whole tree.
  Rate-limited console.warn (max 1 per 5s, no node contents) for diagnostics.
Guard marker `__domRemoveChildGuard` prevents double-install.

or blank flash between cycles — only the two loading screens. Started after the

STRUCTURAL FACTS (verified by read, prior round)

  preview panel reloads it.

SHIPPED THIS ROUND

   every access try/catch-wrapped, forensics can never throw):

     timestamp + cycle counter; a re-boot within 15s logs
